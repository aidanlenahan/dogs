Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 14, 2026

This page explains what information dogs.aidanlenahan.com collects and how it's used. This site is a informational page for Lenahan Dog Services and does not have user accounts, does not process payments, and does not contain any forms that collect personal information.

Analytics
This site uses Google Analytics (GA4) to understand how visitors use the site — for example, which pages are viewed and which buttons (Email, Facebook, GPS info, reviews) are clicked. Google Analytics uses cookies and collects information such as approximate location (derived from IP address), device and browser type, and referring website. This data is aggregated and used only to improve the site; it is not sold or shared with third parties beyond Google's processing of the analytics data. You can read Google's own privacy practices at policies.google.com/privacy.

You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking across all websites using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on, or by blocking cookies/scripts in your browser settings.

Embedded content
The GPS Tracking info section embeds a Strava activity via Strava's embed widget, which may set its own cookies or collect data according to Strava's privacy policy.

Contact
Reaching out via the Email or Facebook links on this site sends your message directly to those platforms (your email client or Facebook) — this site does not store or process that message itself.

Questions
If you have questions about this policy, contact aidan@aidanlenahan.com.
